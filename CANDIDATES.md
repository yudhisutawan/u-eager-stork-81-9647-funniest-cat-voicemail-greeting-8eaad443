# Local candidates: Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting

Judge criteria (in the published order): (1) reads as a cat's voicemail
greeting and lands a laugh; (2) joke specific to cats (naps, boxes,
knocking things off, 4 AM food demands); (3) the cat has a clear voice and
attitude of its own; (4) works spoken aloud, punch arrives quickly;
(5) fresh and surprising, not a worn pun or catchphrase. Max 120 chars.

## L1 (110)
Hi, you've reached Mittens. I'm asleep on your clean laundry. Leave a message — I'll pretend I didn't hear it.
Angle: nap on clean laundry + wilful deafness.

## L2 (112)
Meow. You've reached the cat. I'm busy staring at a wall for no reason. Leave a message. Or don't. I don't care.
Angle: staring at a wall for no reason + indifference. (First draft was
127 chars; trimmed "after the beep" to fit the 120-char gate.)

## L3 (93)
This is Biscuit. I knocked your phone off the table. If it's about the vet, I was never here.
Angle: knocking things off + vet denial.

## L4 (97)
You've reached Whiskers. I'm in the box. The box is mine now. Leave a message, but the box stays.
Angle: box ownership, deadpan claim.

## L5 (117)
Meow. It's 4 AM and the bowl is empty. This is not a drill. Leave a message — preferably one that sounds like kibble.
Angle: 4 AM food demand + "not a drill" urgency + kibble punch.

## L6 (105)
Hi, it's Mochi. I sat on your keyboard and sent your boss an email. Leave a message after the beep. Meow.
Angle: keyboard sabotage escalated to the boss.

## L7 (112)
You've reached the cat. I am currently ignoring you from inside the closet. Leave a message. I'll read it never.
Angle: hiding in the closet + "I'll read it never".

## L8 (109)
Meow. The human is out. I ate the plant. It was delicious. Leave a message after the beep, preferably snacks.
Angle: plant-eating confession + deadpan "delicious".
